Flagship platform

Hostel Management System (HMS).

Purpose-built for worker accommodation management, HMS consolidates company, worker, hostel, bed and inspection records through a structured system.

  • Company and worker management
  • Hostel, block, floor and unit setup
  • Bed availability and allocation
  • Inspection checklists and photo records
  • Role-based access and approvals
  • Operational reports and expiry monitoring
