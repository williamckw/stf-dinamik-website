HMS FAQ

Common Questions

What is a Hostel Management System?

A Hostel Management System is software used to organise accommodation-related information such as rooms, beds, occupants, inspections and reporting.

Can HMS help track worker bed allocation?

The planned platform manages a hierarchy of hostels, blocks, floors, units and beds, with worker allocation records.

Can hostel inspectors add checklist items and photos?

The inspection workflow supports configurable inspection areas/items, findings, remarks and supporting photo records.

Is hostel maintenance included?

Maintenance and inventory workflows are a proposed product expansion, subject to project scope and implementation.
