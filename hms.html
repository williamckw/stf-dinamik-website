Flagship Solution

Hostel Management System (HMS)

A practical digital platform for worker accommodation, hostel records, inspections and operating visibility.

Centralised Hostel Operations

HMS brings company, worker and accommodation records into one platform, with structured workflows for inspections, assignments, approvals and reports.

  • Company and worker management
  • Hostel, block, floor, unit and bed allocation
  • Passport and permit expiry monitoring
  • Inspection forms, remarks and photo evidence
  • Role-based permissions and approvals
