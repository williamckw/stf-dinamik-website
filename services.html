Choosing the Right Business System.

What is the difference between CRM and ERP?

CRM focuses on managing relationships, leads and customer communication; ERP typically integrates business operations such as inventory, procurement and reporting.

Can a custom business application integrate with our current software?

Integration depends on access to supported APIs, data specifications and technical compatibility.

Do you develop mobile-compatible systems?

Responsive web applications can be designed to work across phones, tablets and desktop browsers.

Can we develop the software in phases?
